Made in Common is a home for open source tools made for the web.

Most of these projects began with one person &ndash; but we believe that the best things are made together with others. Each project here has several people looking after it: reviewing changes, answering questions, and thinking carefully before changing something others rely on. Sharing that work means nobody has to carry a project alone, and the tools can stay well cared for over years, whoever happens to be busy this month.
